The Science

How Proliv™Rx Works

Proliv™Rx uses a non-invasive wearable headset with integrated electrodes that contact the scalp, delivering Combined Occipital and Trigeminal Afferent Stimulation (eCOT-AS).

1

Targeted Stimulation

Forehead electrodes stimulate trigeminal nerve branches; rear electrodes target occipital nerve branches.

2

Neural Pathways

Gentle electrical pulses travel through sensory nerves to the brainstem, into thalamo-cortical and limbic networks.

3

Mood Regulation

Signals modulate brain regions involved in mood, arousal, and autonomic regulation to alleviate depression.

Clinical Evidence

The MOOD Study

A multicenter, randomized, double-blind, sham-controlled trial evaluating Proliv™Rx over 16 weeks of home-based self-administration under physician supervision. Results demonstrated significant improvement in depression severity, with 81% of patients classified as severe at baseline reduced to just 15% by week 16.

Your Journey

Proliv™Rx shifts treatment from the clinic to your home while preserving full physician oversight from the experts at Resiliency Mind+Body Medicine.

    Your physician evaluates your history. The device is then delivered directly to your home.
