The Science
How ProlivΓäóRx Works
ProlivΓäóRx uses a non-invasive wearable headset with three pairs of integrated electrodes that
                    contact the scalp on the forehead and occiput, delivering Combined Occipital and Trigeminal Afferent
                    Stimulation (eCOT-AS).
Forehead electrodes stimulate the
                        supraorbital and supratrochlear trigeminal nerve branches bilaterally.
Rear electrodes stimulate the two
                        greater occipital nerve branches at the back of the head.
Electrical pulses travel through sensory
                        nerves to the brainstem, then into thalamo-cortical and limbic networks.
Signals modulate brain regions involved
                        in mood, arousal, and autonomic regulation to alleviate depression.
